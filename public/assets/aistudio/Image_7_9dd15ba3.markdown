## Brand & Style

### Personality & Purpose
The design system drives a civic transformation within national vocational education (SMK) and youth workforce readiness. The brand personality balances **institutional trust, civic rigor, and dynamic youth empowerment**. It steers away from dated bureaucratic interfaces, adopting the clarity and efficiency of premier digital public services (such as Gov.uk and GovTech Singapore) while maintaining a warm, actionable, and aspirational cadence for teenage learners, career center counselors (BKK), and enterprise partners.

### Design Style: Modern Civic Bento & Frosted Elevation
The visual direction fuses **Modern Civic Utilitarianism** with **Glassmorphism and Bento-Grid Modularity**:
- **Structured Precision**: High-density data dashboards, verifiable credential layouts, and modular tracking components aligned along mathematically strict grids.
- **Controlled Vibrancy**: Grounded, high-contrast surfaces paired with four precise pillar-driven chromatic gradients representing vocational stages.
- **Tactile Trust**: Micro-glow badges, fine translucent glass outlines (`1px rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` to `rgba(15,23,42,0.08)`), and soft layered elevations that evoke modern cryptographic identity cards and tangible field certificates.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around four functional vocational pillars anchored by a deep civic navy substrate for operational efficiency, paired with an accessible high-contrast public mode.

### Four Core Pillars
- **Pillar 1 — Magang (Apprenticeship & Practical Action):** Primary Amber to Warm Orange (`#F59E0B` to `#EA580C`). Communicates industrious energy, on-site execution, and vocational momentum.
- **Pillar 2 — Identitas (Digital Credentialing & Trust):** Primary Blue to Indigo (`#3B82F6` to `#6366F1`). Establishes cryptographic certainty, verified NISN/NIK state identity, and institutional endorsement.
- **Pillar 3 — Tamatan (Graduate Tracer & Accountability):** Emerald to Deep Teal (`#10B981` to `#14B8A6`). Symbolizes employment outcomes, real-time analytics, and workforce absorption.
- **Pillar 4 — Rujukan & Asistensi (Mentorship & Care):** Violet to Vibrant Magenta (`#8B5CF6` to `#D946EF`). Conveys human counseling, psycho-social career support, and institutional guidance.

### Surface Architecture
- **Dashboard / Operational Console Mode (Default):**
  - Base Background: Deep Space Navy (`#0B1020`)
  - Elevated Card Surface: Midnight Navy (`#121A33`)
  - Hover & Active Container: Slate Navy Highlight (`#1A2444`)
  - Borders: Translucent Structural Rim (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)`)
- **Public / Portal Mode:**
  - Base Background: Crisp Alabaster Slate (`#F8FAFC`)
  - Elevated Card Surface: Pure Opaque White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Hover / Sub-layer: Subtle Mist (`#F1F5F9`)
  - Borders: Pale Steel Border (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`)

### Contrast & State Standards
All critical interactive foreground elements strictly adhere to **WCAG 2.1 AA** standards (4.5:1 minimum contrast for normal text; 3:1 for large display elements and graphical badges).

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dichotomy between human optimism and digital-public integrity:

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings & Body UI):** Delivers clean geometry with warm, humanist finishes. It excels at complex Indonesian syllabic pacing and administrative titles, eliminating institutional coldness.
- **JetBrains Mono (Identifiers, Tokens & Metrics):** Applied strictly to tabular telemetry, serial identifiers, blockchain/credential hashes, live tracer counters, and validation status pills (e.g., `PILOT-SIMULATION-DATA`, `VERIFIED-NISN-32049102`).
- **Typographic Treatment:** Numerical counters and analytics dashboards must activate `tnum` (tabular figures) across all font roles to avoid alignment jitter during real-time data updates.

## Layout & Spacing

### Bento-Grid & Modular Framework
The architecture uses a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed specifically for modular bento-style grouping:
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2rem` to `3rem`. Bento modules span varying configurations (col-span-3, 4, 6, 8, 12) accommodating asymmetrical tracer KPIs and dual verification panels.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.5rem`. Complex asymmetric bento rows collapse into 4x4 or 8x8 pairs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter: 0.75rem`, `margin: 1rem`. Bento panels reflow linearly into vertical priority order: Verification State -> Action Item -> Telemetry Cards.

### Spatial Rhythm
Layout rhythms observe an 8px basal increment. Card internal padding is standardized to `space-lg` (24px) for desktop telemetry modules and `space-md` (16px) for mobile surfaces. Element-to-label gaps maintain a dense `space-xs` (4px) or `space-sm` (8px) relationship to retain high scanning efficiency.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use murky, dark drop shadows. Instead, it relies on **photonic micro-glows, tonal stacking, and translucent borders**.

### Elevation Scales
- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas Base):**
  - Dark: `#0B1020`
  - Public: `#F8FAFC`
- **Surface Level 1 (Bento Cards & Data Panels):**
  - Dark: `#121A33` with border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
  - Public: `#FFFFFF` with border `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`
  - Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)` (Dark) / `0 4px 18px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` (Public)
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Floating Modules, Flyouts & Modals):**
  - Dark: `#1A2444` with border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`
  - Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)` with ambient pilar glow
- **Micro-Glow Badges & Active States:**
  - P1 Magang: Soft rim shadow `0 0 16px -2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`
  - P2 Identitas: Soft rim shadow `0 0 16px -2px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.35)`
  - P3 Tamatan: Soft rim shadow `0 0 16px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`
  - P4 Rujukan: Soft rim shadow `0 0 16px -2px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)`
- **Glass Backdrops (Filter & Sticky Nav):**
  - Dark: `rgba(11, 16, 32, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
  - Public: `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.8)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded-2xl modularity** that feels friendly yet refined:
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** 16px (`1rem`) to 20px (`1.25rem`) corner radiuses create a modern smartphone-native hardware aesthetic.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields:** Standard 8px (`0.5rem`) to 12px (`0.75rem`) radiuses ensure tactile button targets while preventing button-to-card visual conflicts.
- **Pillar Tags & Cryptographic Indicators:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) rounded profiles for metadata tags, simulation watermarks, and verification states.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Brand / Pillar specific):** Solid vibrant fill using the respective pillar accent or `#3B82F6` as primary default. Crisp white label (`Plus Jakarta Sans 600`), 10px corner radius, 12px vertical padding. Active state features a soft inward pressure transform (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Dark mode features `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)` background with a `rgba(255,255,255,0.12)` border. On hover, shifts to `rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`.
- **Institutional Action (Verify / Sign):** Accompanied by mono-spaced cryptographic indicators and subtle chevron icon indicators.

### Micro-Glow Badges & Status Pills
- **Pilot Token Pill:** Always prefixed with a pulsing terminal dot. Example: `● DATA SIMULASI PILOT` in `JetBrains Mono 10px`, tracked at `0.05em`. Background: `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#F59E0B`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`.
- **Pillar Identifiers:** Compact pill badges matching respective pillar color sets (e.g., `PILAR 02 // IDENTITAS` with `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.15)` background and `#60A5FA` text).

### Cards & Bento Panels
- Structured with internal 24px padding (`space-lg`), subtle hairline borders, and localized ambient lighting.
- Top-right corner reserved for `JetBrains Mono` reference chips or live status indicators.
- In dark mode, card header divides from content with a hairline gradient separator (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 44px (touch friendly). Dark mode background `#0E1428`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`. Focus state introduces a crisp `0 0 0 2px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.5)` halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded 4px (checkboxes) and full circle (radios). Checked fill leverages the designated operational pillar color, showing high contrast checks.

### Lists & Modular Tables
- Alternating subtle rows (`#121A33` and `#151E3B` in dark mode).
- Monospaced serial tags (e.g., student NISN, internship batch code) anchored to the leftmost column in `JetBrains Mono`.
- Interactive hover effects trigger horizontal row highlighting with an accent boundary indicator on the left rim.
